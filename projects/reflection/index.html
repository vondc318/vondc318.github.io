Reflection

What I’m noticing so far

One thing I am noticing about my submissions is the way they encourage viewers to think. I don’t like to lay things out clearly, I would rather engage users in a way that is meaningful. The most recent project, our visual only website, accomplished this by allowing viewers to click through imagery that leads to different web pages. It is not explicitly stated that this is how to interact with my website but as you explore the page you get to discover new pieces of visual information. I think each of my projects are all visually different, but maintain this feeling of exploration. I have learned a lot about how to organize and place assets in the medium of a website, whether it is static images or animated ones. In each of the projects I have found myself referencing images, which I find to be one of the most important interactions for building html websites. I would love to experiment more with motion and animations, as well as incorporating sound.
